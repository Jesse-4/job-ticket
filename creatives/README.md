# Job Ticket: TikTok creatives

Eight finished vertical videos, a cover for each, five scripts to film yourself, and a two-week posting plan.

Everything shown in the videos is the real app (`index.html`) being driven step by step: real searches, real size questions, real totals, and the actual PDF the app produced. Every price on screen is the app's own math from that trade's default rates. Business and customer names are made-up examples. No reviews, testimonials, user counts or stats appear anywhere.

All are 1080×1920, H.264 + AAC, 30 fps, about 2–3 MB each, **with a voiceover**. The same videos without the voiceover (sound effects only) are in `videos/no-voiceover/`. Covers are in `covers/` with the same names (1080×1920 PNG).

**Safe zone.** Captions sit between about y=200 and y=1440 and stay out of the right 15%, so TikTok's buttons and caption text don't cover them. The phone can extend into the bottom area. That's fine, since nothing there needs reading.

**Audio.** Every video has a voiceover that walks through what's on screen, timed to each caption, over light sound effects (taps, key clicks, a "ding" on send). The voice and the effects were generated for these videos; there's no music and nothing sampled.
- **Voiced version (`videos/`):** post as is, or add a trending sound under it at about 10–20% so the voice stays clear.
- **No-voiceover version (`videos/no-voiceover/`):** use it when you want a trending sound front and center, or to record your own voice over it in TikTok (Edit → Voiceover). Your own voice usually beats a generated one, and the scripts below are written to be read aloud.
- **Label the AI voice.** The voiceover is synthetic (Kokoro, an open-source text-to-speech model). Turn on **"AI-generated content"** under the post's settings (More options) when you upload the voiced versions. TikTok asks creators to label realistic AI-made audio, and the label doesn't hurt reach the way a takedown would.

**Before posting:** put the app link (`https://jesse-4.github.io/job-ticket/`) in your bio. Every video ends on "Link in bio".

## The videos

### 1. Speed run: water heater quote (plumbers)
**What happens:** a stopwatch starts. Setup takes the rate and business name, then a search for "water heater", the job priced from your rate, the customer name, Send, Share PDF. The clock stops and the actual PDF slides in.

**Caption:**
> Timed myself writing a water heater quote on my phone. Setup included ⏱️ Priced from my own hourly rate and markup, sent as a PDF. 3 free invoices, quotes are always free. Link in bio.

**Hashtags:** #plumber #plumbinglife #contractor #smallbusinesstips #invoice #tradesman

**Sound:** an upbeat, fast-ticking or "speedrun" style trending beat. Anything with a strong pulse that matches the stopwatch.

**Why the hook works:** "Clock starts now" creates a countdown people want to see finish, and the running timer is a reason to stay to the end. "Plumbers" in the corner tells the target viewer in under a second that it's for them.

### 2. Carbon pad vs. the app (electricians, all trades)
**What happens:** a scribbled carbon-pad quote with crossed-out math ("math done in the truck", "can the customer even read it?"). Then the same kind of job in the app: search "outlets", answer one question (8 outlets), and it does the math. Ends on the pad and the real PDF side by side, BEFORE / AFTER.

**Caption:**
> If your quotes still live on a carbon pad, this one's for you. Search the job, answer one question, send a clean PDF. Quotes are always free. Link in bio.

**Hashtags:** #electrician #electricianlife #bluecollar #contractorlife #smallbusiness #tradesman

**Sound:** a "glow-up" or transformation sound. A beat that drops right when the app appears (about 3.5 s) works best.

**Why the hook works:** almost every tradesperson recognizes that pad. Recognition plus mild embarrassment ("…on *one of these?*") stops the scroll, and the before/after promises a payoff they'll wait for.

### 3. POV: 10:47 PM and the invoices aren't sent (broad)
**What happens:** a dark-mode phone screen with the clock reading 10:47. + New → Invoice → "deep clean" → 4 bedrooms → customer → Send → PDF. "Sent. Still 10:47."

**Caption:**
> The worst part of running your own business is the paperwork after the work 😮‍💨 Made this so invoices take a minute instead of an evening. Link in bio, 3 invoices free.

**Hashtags:** #smallbusinessowner #cleaningbusiness #entrepreneurlife #sidehustle #invoice #pov

**Sound:** a calm, late-night lo-fi sound, or a trending "tired/relatable" audio.

**Why the hook works:** POV plus a specific, uncomfortable time is classic relatable content. Anyone who bills clients has done invoices in bed. The real dark mode and the 10:47 clock make it feel like a real screen recording, not an ad.

### 4. Who owes you money right now? (broad)
**What happens:** All tickets with example data: owed to you, overdue flagged red, open quotes, collected. Tap Overdue to see who to call, open one, mark it Paid (PAID stamp), and the totals update.

**Caption:**
> If "who still owes me?" means scrolling through old texts, you're leaving money out there. Every invoice, what's late, and what's been paid in one screen. Link in bio.

**Hashtags:** #smallbusinesstips #getpaid #landscaping #lawncare #businessowner #invoice

**Sound:** a suspenseful "quick question…" or "be honest" style audio, or a subtle, tense beat.

**Why the hook works:** a direct question the viewer can't answer instantly makes them stay for the answer. The second line ("let me check my texts…") is the relatable gut-punch.

### 5. Things I wish I knew when I started my business (new owners)
**What happens:** four pricing lessons, each shown in the app. (1) Price from an hourly rate, and watch the worked example recalculate. (2) Mark up materials. (3) Set a minimum charge. (4) Put the price in writing first: a deck stain quote for 450 sq ft.

**Caption:**
> 4 pricing lessons that would've saved me a lot of free work. Which one did you learn the hard way? 👇

**Hashtags:** #thingsiwishiknew #smallbusinesstips #startingabusiness #painter #pricing #entrepreneur

**Sound:** a reflective or "storytime" trending audio, or soft piano.

**Why the hook works:** "Things I wish I knew" is a proven listicle format. It promises quick, useful value, and numbered points create an open loop ("what's #4?"). The caption question invites comments, which push distribution.

### 6. You're losing money on small jobs (handymen, all trades)
**What happens:** a quick estimate for "fix sticky door", 0.5 hr plus $8 materials, comes to $41.70. The app warns it's $58.30 under the $100 minimum. One tap brings it up, now $107.25 with tax. All figures are the app's own math at the Handyman defaults.

**Caption:**
> A 30-minute job still costs you the drive, the gas and half your afternoon. Set a minimum and stick to it. The app warns you when a quote's under it. Link in bio.

**Hashtags:** #handyman #handymanbusiness #pricingtips #contractor #smallbusiness #bluecollar

**Sound:** an "oh no" or "wait for it" trending audio that lands on the red warning (about 8 s).

**Why the hook works:** loss aversion. "You're losing money" is more compelling than "make more money", and "small jobs" is specific enough that every handyman thinks of one.

### 7. Starting a pressure washing side hustle? (side hustlers)
**What happens:** setup from scratch (Pressure washing, "Fresh Start Washing"), then a driveway quote with the size question (900 sq ft), sent as a PDF with the business name on top. "Quotes are always free."

**Caption:**
> First pressure washing customer? Send them a real quote, not a text that says "$150?" Free for quotes, 3 invoices free. Link in bio.

**Hashtags:** #pressurewashing #sidehustle #powerwashing #startingabusiness #smallbusiness #sidehustleideas

**Sound:** a hype or "starting my business" montage sound.

**Why the hook works:** an identity callout ("Starting a pressure washing side hustle?") makes the exact viewer feel spoken to, and pressure washing is one of the most popular side hustles on TikTok. "Look legit from day one" names the insecurity new owners have.

### 8. Another app with a monthly fee? No thanks. (everyone)
**What happens:** a "No thanks." stamp, a fast AC tune-up quote sent as a PDF, then what you get (works with no signal, home screen, data stays on your phone, quotes always free, 3 invoices free), then "$29 once. One-time founding price. No monthly fee."

**Caption:**
> Didn't want another subscription just to send invoices. Quotes are free, 3 invoices free, then $29 once. That's it. Link in bio.

**Hashtags:** #hvac #hvactech #smallbusiness #nosubscription #contractor #invoice

**Sound:** a confident, punchy "no thanks" or trending "I said what I said" style audio.

**Why the hook works:** subscription fatigue is real and universal. The stamp gives a clear point of view in the first second, and the price reveal at the end is the payoff.

## Scripts to film yourself

Creator-style clips often beat polished ones. Film on your phone, vertical, natural light. Use the app's real screen for any phone shots (screen-record, or film the phone over your shoulder). Keep the on-screen text inside the safe zone: upper two-thirds, away from the right edge.

### Script A: "Quote from the driveway" (hands-on-phone, ~20 s)
**Setting:** standing next to your truck or in a customer's driveway (no customer's face or address visible).

| Time | Shot | You say | On-screen text |
|------|------|---------|----------------|
| 0–2 s | Close on your hand pulling the phone from a pocket, truck behind | "Customer wants a price before I leave. Watch." | Customer wants a price NOW |
| 2–10 s | Over-the-shoulder: search the job, answer the size question | "Search the job, how many… done. It's priced off my rate." | Priced from MY rate |
| 10–15 s | Tap Send → Share PDF → pick Messages | "Send. That's a real quote, not a number on a napkin." | Sent before I'm in the truck |
| 15–20 s | Face to camera, phone in hand | "Quotes are free. Link's in my bio." | Link in bio · quotes always free |

### Script B: "The job I undercharged" (face to camera, ~25 s)
**Setting:** truck cab or kitchen table. One take, no cuts needed.

| Time | Shot | You say | On-screen text |
|------|------|---------|----------------|
| 0–3 s | Face, close | "I once charged forty bucks for a job that took my whole afternoon." | I charged $40 for this 😬 |
| 3–12 s | Face | "Thirty minutes of work. But I drove across town, sat in traffic, and lost a bigger job." | 30 min of work ≠ 30 min of your day |
| 12–20 s | Cut to screen recording: quick estimate → minimum warning → "Bring it up to the minimum" | "Now my app won't let me forget my minimum. It flags it, one tap fixes it." | Set a minimum. Every time. |
| 20–25 s | Face | "What's your minimum? Tell me below." | What's YOUR minimum? 👇 |

(Only say the $40 story if it's true for you. Otherwise use a real one of your own.)

### Script C: "Reply to a comment: how do you price jobs?" (green screen, ~25 s)
**Setting:** TikTok's green screen effect with a screenshot of the app's **My rates** page behind you. Use it to reply to a real comment once you have one.

| Time | Shot | You say | On-screen text |
|------|------|---------|----------------|
| 0–3 s | You over the screenshot, pointing | "Here's exactly how I price every job." | How I price every job |
| 3–10 s | Point at hourly rate | "Hours times my rate. Not what the last guy charged." | 1. Hours × your rate |
| 10–16 s | Point at markup | "Plus a markup on parts, because I drove to get them." | 2. Mark up materials |
| 16–21 s | Point at minimum | "And a minimum, because small jobs still cost me the trip." | 3. Have a minimum |
| 21–25 s | Face | "The app I use does the math. Link in bio." | Link in bio |

### Script D: "Day 1 of my cleaning business" (series starter, ~20 s)
**Setting:** car or kitchen, supplies visible. Works for any trade; swap the job.

| Time | Shot | You say | On-screen text |
|------|------|---------|----------------|
| 0–2 s | Supplies on the counter | "Day one of my cleaning business. First quote." | Day 1 of my cleaning business |
| 2–12 s | Screen record: setup (Cleaning, rate, name) → "deep clean" → bedrooms | "Picked my trade, set my rate, three bedrooms…" | Setup took 30 seconds |
| 12–17 s | Screen record: the PDF | "…and it looks like I've been doing this for years." | Looks legit ✅ |
| 17–20 s | Face | "Follow along. Day two, I find out if they said yes." | Day 2 tomorrow → |

### Script E: "What I send instead of a text" (hands only, ~15 s)
**Setting:** phone on a tailgate or workbench, filmed from above.

| Time | Shot | You say | On-screen text |
|------|------|---------|----------------|
| 0–3 s | A text bubble typed out: "it'll be about 350" | "Stop sending prices like this." | Stop quoting like this ❌ |

## Two-week posting plan

Post once a day at the times your audience is off work. For trades that's usually **6–8 AM** (before the first job) or **7–9 PM**; for side hustlers and new owners, evenings. Pick one slot for week 1 and try the other in week 2.

| Day | Post | What it tests |
|-----|------|---------------|
| 1 | **1. Speed run (plumbers)** | Baseline: trade-specific + speed hook |
| 2 | **3. POV 10:47 PM** | Broad, relatable pain hook |
| 3 | **2. Carbon pad vs. app** | Before/after format |
| 4 | **Script A** (filmed by you) | Creator-style vs. polished |
| 5 | **4. Who owes you** | Getting-paid angle |
| 6 | **6. Small jobs** | Money-left-on-the-table angle |
| 7 | **5. Things I wish I knew** | Listicle, comment bait |
| 8 | Repost the **week 1 winner** from `videos/no-voiceover/` with a trending sound (or your own voice) instead of the generated voice | Voice vs. no voice: which keeps people watching |
| 9 | **7. Side hustle** | Side-hustle audience |
| 10 | **Script B** (filmed) | Face-to-camera storytelling |
| 11 | **8. No monthly fee** | Price-led hook |
| 12 | **Script D**, day 1 of a series | Does a series earn follows? |
| 13 | **Script C** as a reply to a real comment (or **Script E** if none yet) | Comment replies |
| 14 | Remix of the overall winner for a **different trade** (e.g. speed run as an electrician) | Does the format travel across trades? |

### What to watch (TikTok Studio → each post's analytics)
- **First 2–3 seconds kept (retention graph):** the hook test. A big drop in the first second means the hook or cover failed. Rewrite the first line, not the whole video.
- **Average watch time and % watched in full:** for 15–25 s videos, aim for over 50% of the length. If most people leave mid-way, tighten the middle.
- **Rewatches:** loops mean the payoff (the PDF, the PAID stamp, the price) landed. Repeat that format.
- **Profile visits:** the clearest sign of buying interest. Compare profile visits per 1,000 views between videos, not raw views.
- **Link clicks (bio):** the real goal. Track with a link shortener or UTM on the bio link (e.g. `?utm_source=tiktok&utm_content=v01`), switching `utm_content` when the day's video goes up so you can tell which one drove clicks.
- **Comments and shares:** trade-specific videos tend to get "what about X?" comments. Reply to them with Script C-style videos.

### Simple rules
- Don't judge a video before **48 hours**.
- If a video gets **2× your median profile visits**, make two more in the same format for different trades.
- If one hook style beats the others twice, make it 50% of week 3.
- Keep reusing what works: the same video with a new first line and a new sound is a valid new post.

## Regenerating the videos

The renderer lives in `creatives/src/`. It drives the real app inside a phone frame, frame by frame, and pipes screenshots into ffmpeg.

```bash
# from the repo root
python3 -m http.server 8765 &        # the renderer loads the app from here
cd creatives/src
./render-all.sh                      # all videos (about 3–4 minutes), or ./render-all.sh 03 for one
PREVIEW=1 node videos/03-pov-late-invoices.js   # quick stills to build/snaps, no video
```

Needs Node with Playwright/Chromium, ffmpeg, Python 3 with numpy and Pillow, and `pdftoppm` (poppler). For the voiceover: `pip install kokoro-onnx soundfile`, download `kokoro-v1.0.onnx` and `voices-v1.0.bin` from the [kokoro-onnx releases](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases), and set `JT_KOKORO_DIR` to their folder. Lines are spoken with `v.say("…")` in the video scripts, cached in `build/vo/`, and `VO_REPORT=1` prints any place where the picture holds while a line finishes. `stage.html` is the canvas (phone frame, captions, end card), `engine.js` is the driver, `sound.py` synthesizes the sound effects and mixes the voice (ducking the effects under it), `tts.py` speaks one line, and `videos/*.js` are the eight scripts. Intermediate files go to `creatives/build/` (ignored by git).
